01 / About

Not a women’s magazine.
Not a self-help corner.
A room with the door open.

The Women’s Question borrows its name from an old argument: what, exactly, should women be allowed to be?

We are less interested in answering that question than in changing who gets to ask it.

This is a space for candor, disagreement, curiosity, confession, criticism, solidarity, and the occasional bad opinion said out loud.
